How it works

Evaluations decide what we collect next.

A policy that works in your lab can fail on a new object, in different light or in an unfamiliar room. Evaluation findings guide targeted collection; the next evaluation tests what improved and what still fails.

01

Define

We agree on the task, the robot, the target environments and how success is measured.

  • Task and success criteria
  • Robots and target environments
  • Evaluation plan

Output

A plan: what to collect, where, and how success is judged.

02

Collect

Trained operators teleoperate real robots across varied environments to capture targeted demonstrations.

  • Remote teleoperation by qualified operators
  • Varied objects, light and rooms
  • Wrist and scene cameras

Output

Teleoperated episodes of your task, recorded across the target environments.

03

Verify

Episodes are reviewed for outcome, failure modes and severity. A failed attempt can still be useful data.

04

Train

Your lab trains or fine-tunes on the verified dataset, or we help you train.

  • Only reviewed, usable episodes
  • Train in-house or with our help
  • Fine-tune on targeted data

Output

An updated policy, ready for real-robot evaluation.

05

Evaluate

We run the updated policy on real robots and analyze how and where it fails.

  • Trials on robots around the world
  • Failure analysis
  • Agreed evaluation criteria

Output

A failure report: where the policy fails, and how.

06

Repeat

Evaluation findings decide the next targeted collection.

  • Collect what failed
  • Operators qualified for the task
  • Measure what improved

Output

The next targeted collection, scoped from the evaluation findings.

Why the data holds up

Operators qualify by robot, task and interface, including accuracy, latency and safety. Labeling and episode QA assess outcomes, failure modes and severity against your program’s criteria, so what you receive is a usable dataset.
